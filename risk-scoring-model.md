# Cloud Risk Scoring Model

## Likelihood (1–5)
1 — Rare  
2 — Unlikely  
3 — Possible  
4 — Likely  
5 — Almost Certain  

## Impact (1–5)
1 — Minimal  
2 — Moderate  
3 — Significant  
4 — Major  
5 — Critical  

## Risk Score Formula
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

## Risk Levels
1–7 = Low  
8–14 = Medium  
15–25 = High
